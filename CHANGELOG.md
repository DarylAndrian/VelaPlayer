# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-25

Dailymotion support, with ad-free streaming.

- Paste, share, or tap a Dailymotion link (video, dai.ly short, embed, and geo-player links) and VelaPlayer opens it in the downloader
- "Watch now · no ads" streams the video straight from its HLS stream, with seeking, speed control, and picture-in-picture
- Expired stream links are refreshed once automatically and playback resumes where it stopped
- Dailymotion videos download as 360p, 720p, 1080p, or audio only, and show a Dailymotion label in the gallery
- Clearer errors: an out-of-date yt-dlp engine now says so and points to Settings → Update yt-dlp engine, and network failures are called out separately
- Added HLS playback support (Media3 HLS module) to the player

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-22

First public release of VelaPlayer.

- Combined YouTube and Instagram downloader for 360p, 720p, 1080p, audio only, and Instagram photos
- Share-sheet import, in-app Instagram login, and a foreground download notification
- Offline gallery that opens on starred items, with search, type filters, and a device video scan
- Offline player with a seek bar, labeled 10-second skips, and double-tap seek on the left and right halves
- Previous and next controls, plus a queue that plays in order, shuffles, or repeats the current video
- Playback speeds from 1x through 10x, a screen lock, picture-in-picture, and a rotate button
- Light and dark Emerald Slate theme
